Smile for Lidiya: Support Her Journey

Accommodations, Care, Routine Speech Therapy, Equipment, and Future Surgeries for a Brighter Smile

About Lidiya

My name is Tias Adiyatna, and I am the father of Lidiya Safira Miyuki, our first and only daughter. She was born with cleft lip and cleft palate, which makes it difficult for her to eat, speak, and breathe normally.

Every day, our hearts ache watching her little struggles just to drink milk or try to smile like other babies. When the doctor confirmed her diagnosis after birth, we were devastated. But we know this is a test, and we must stay strong for Lidiya.

Our Journey So Far

For the past several months, our lives have revolved around hospital visits, consultations with doctors, and searching for ways to support her. We have already completed her first surgery, and we are now preparing for the second. The first and second surgeries are covered by national health insurance, similar to public health coverage programs abroad like Medicaid or NHS.

However, the ongoing costs for transportation, routine check-ups, medications, and special feeding equipment have drained our savings. Even though my wife and I work and sell goods on weekends and after hours, it's challenging to keep up.

We have borrowed from friends and family and taken out loans, but we still need support for the road ahead.

Why We Need Support

Lidiya has already undergone her first surgery (labiocheiloplasty - cleft lip repair), and we are heading toward the second (palatoplasty - cleft palate repair). After the second surgery, future procedures—such as a third or fourth surgery that may be needed as she grows—will not be covered by insurance and must be paid out-of-pocket.

This campaign is not to force or pressure anyone into donating; it's entirely voluntary. Any help is appreciated from the heart. The funds will primarily go toward:

  • Accommodations during hospital stays
  • Ongoing care and rehabilitation
  • Special medical equipment and tools
  • Routine weekly speech therapy after the second surgery
  • Potential third or fourth surgeries in the future

Without adequate support, Lidiya may continue to face:

  • Challenges with eating and drinking
  • Risk of malnutrition and delayed growth
  • Breathing difficulties
  • Future speech issues
  • Recurring infections
  • Social and emotional challenges

Estimated Costs

The total estimated need for accommodations, care, equipment, and future surgeries is approximately USD $15,000. This includes:

  • Travel and lodging during treatments
  • Post-operative care and therapy
  • Routine weekly speech therapy
  • Medications and specialized equipment
  • Future uncovered surgeries and rehabilitation

We don't have the exact total yet, but this amount will help cover the essential ongoing and future needs.

💖 A Father's Humble Request

As a father, I feel saddened that I can't provide everything my daughter needs on my own. I've tried every possible way, but today, I humbly share our story. If you feel moved to help voluntarily, it would mean the world to us.

We appreciate any support, but please know it's entirely up to you.
